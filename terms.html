Terms & Conditions

These are the working terms for freelance website and web application projects with Praveen Kumar. Please read them before confirming a project, and ask me about anything that is unclear.

  1. 1. Quotation and Validity

    • A written quotation will be shared after we discuss the project scope. It will describe the agreed features, the cost and the estimated timeline.
    • The quotation states how long it remains valid. After that period, the cost or timeline may need to be reviewed before work begins.

  2. 2. Project Confirmation

    • The client must discuss and confirm the project requirements.
    • Development starts only after the client approves the quotation and the project scope. Approval can be given in writing by email or WhatsApp.

  3. 3. Payment Policy

    • 50% advance payment is required after the client approves the quotation and confirms the project.
    • The remaining 50% must be paid before final website deployment.
    • Final deployment and handover will only happen after receiving 100% of the agreed project amount.
    • The advance payment confirms the project booking and allows development to begin.

  4. 4. Payment Confirmation

    • A payment is treated as received when it reaches the account or method agreed in the quotation.
    • Please share proof of payment, such as a transaction reference, so that it can be matched to the project.

  5. 5. Scope Changes and Revisions

    • Revisions must remain within the originally agreed project scope.
    • Additional features, major design changes and new requirements may involve additional charges.
    • Any additional cost or timeline change will be discussed and approved by the client before work on it proceeds.

  6. 6. Client Responsibilities

    • The client provides the content, text, images, logos and product details needed for the project, and confirms that it may be used.
    • The client gives feedback and approvals in a reasonable time, so the project can stay on schedule.
    • Where needed, the client provides access to existing accounts, such as a domain, hosting or an API, through a secure method agreed for the project.

  7. 7. Project Timelines

    • The estimated timeline will be communicated before development.
    • Delays in receiving content, images, approvals or payments from the client may affect the delivery date.

  8. 8. Testing and Acceptance

    • Before delivery, I test the website on desktop and mobile sizes and check its main features.
    • The client reviews the website and shares feedback. Issues within the agreed scope are corrected before final deployment.
    • The client is responsible for reviewing the final website and confirming that the agreed requirements have been met before deployment.
    • Final deployment takes place after the client has approved the website and the full agreed amount has been received.

  9. 9. Intellectual Property and Source Code

    • Source code and project files are handed over according to the agreed project arrangement, after the full agreed payment is received.
    • Third-party libraries, templates, fonts, images and services remain under their own licences and terms.

  10. 10. Third-Party Costs

    • Domain registration, premium hosting, paid APIs, third-party subscriptions and other external service costs are not included unless explicitly mentioned in the quotation.
    • The client is responsible for applicable third-party charges.
    • I am not responsible for interruptions, price changes or service failures caused by third-party hosting providers, domain registrars or external services.

  11. 11. Cancellation and Refunds

    • If the client cancels after development has started, payment for completed work and any agreed non-refundable expenses will be calculated.
    • Any refundable balance, if applicable, will be discussed and settled based on the work completed and the agreed quotation.
    • Approved additional expenses already paid to third-party providers may not be refundable.

  12. 12. Maintenance and Post-Launch Support

    • Ongoing maintenance, hosting management, updates and additional support are not automatically included.
    • These services can be discussed and quoted separately.

  13. 13. Portfolio Display

    • Unless the client asks in writing before work begins that the project be kept confidential, I may show the project name, screenshots and a short description in my portfolio.

By confirming a project, the client agrees to these Terms & Conditions.

These terms are a plain-language summary of how I work with clients. They are not legal advice. Please see the Privacy Policy for how enquiry details are handled.
