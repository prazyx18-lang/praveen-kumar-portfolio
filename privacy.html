Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal details this portfolio collects, how they are used, and who else may handle them. It is written in plain language.

  1. 1. What this website collects

    • The portfolio itself does not have accounts, sign-in, or a database. It does not store the details you type into the enquiry form.
    • The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, project type and project description. These details are used only to build the message you send.

  2. 2. How an enquiry is sent

    • When you press the enquiry button, WhatsApp opens with your message already written. Nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp.
    • WhatsApp handles the message under its own terms and privacy policy. Once the message reaches WhatsApp, it is delivered to my WhatsApp account.
    • You can also contact me directly by email at prazyx18@gmail.com. Your email is then handled by your email provider and mine.

  3. 3. How your details are used

    • I use the details you send to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and discuss your project.
    • I do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

  4. 4. Cookies, Analytics and Third-Party Services

    • This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or include analytics tracking code.
    • The website does not use cookies for user accounts or personalised advertising.
    • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser contacts Google's font servers, which may process basic connection information under Google's own policies.
    • The hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as IP addresses and server logs, to operate and protect the website.
    • Links to WhatsApp, email and live project websites open external services that have their own privacy policies.

  5. 5. How Long I Keep Enquiry Details

    • I keep enquiry messages only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage agreed projects and maintain relevant project records.
    • You can contact me to request deletion of your enquiry details, subject to any legal or legitimate record-keeping requirements.

  6. 6. Your choices

    • You do not have to use the enquiry form. You can contact me directly on WhatsApp or by email instead.
    • To ask what I hold about you, to correct it, or to ask for its deletion, send me a message on WhatsApp or by email.

  7. 7. Changes to this policy

    • If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

This is a plain-language summary of how this portfolio works. It is not legal advice.

Privacy questions? Contact me on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab) or by email.
